Why Choose the H420

Where Aluminum Meets Ambition

The H420 isn't a compromise between performance and comfort. It's the result of rethinking what a 42-foot yacht should do — starting with the hull and ending at the fly bridge. Here's why serious buyers choose it over conventional GRP alternatives.

01
⬡
Military-Grade Hull Construction

The entire hull, deck, and structural frames are fabricated from 5083-H116 aluminum magnesium alloy — the same specification used in naval and offshore patrol vessels. Panel thickness ranges from 4mm to 10mm depending on load zone, backed by a full longitudinal and transverse stringer system.

vs GRP: 40% lighter · corrosion-proof · structurally superior
02
⚡
Twin Outboard Flexibility

Factory-configured for twin outboard engines from 300HP to 425HP per engine — giving you up to 850HP total. Choose your preferred Mercury or Yamaha power package. Outboard configuration means dramatically reduced maintenance costs, easier servicing, and superior shallow-water access compared to shaft-drive alternatives.

vs inboard: lower cost · quicker service · better draft
03
🧭
LEWMAR Bow Thruster Standard

A LEWMAR® TT 185 Gen 2 12V electric bow thruster is fitted as standard — not an option. Single-handed docking in tight marina berths, crosswinds, or strong tidal currents is transformed from stressful to effortless. Many competing 42-foot vessels offer this only as a costly upgrade.

vs competitors: standard fit · 3.0kW thrust · Gen 2 precision
04
🏠
Genuine Live-Aboard Comfort

Two full private cabins, two TACMA® electric heads with molded FRP walk-in showers, a fully appointed galley, and four 16,000 BTU reverse-cycle marine air conditioning units. The H420 sleeps and lives like a much larger yacht, with synthetic wood flooring, quality upholstery, and GROHE® fittings throughout.

vs comparable length: 4× AC units · 2 full heads · real galley
05
⚡
MASTERVOLT Electrical Architecture

Six dedicated 12V/250AH sealed gel battery banks — separate circuits for house, bow thruster/windlass, and engines. A MASTERVOLT® COMBIMASTER 3000W inverter/charger and VICTRON® galvanic isolator protect your investment. Professional-grade power management typically found on yachts twice the size.

vs standard: 1500AH total · isolated circuits · shore power ready
06
🔒
Safety-First Design Philosophy

Vacuum-infused FRP composite hardtop cored with high-density PVC foam, six through-bolted 316 SS cleats, six automatic bilge pumps with float switches plus two manual emergency pumps, LEWMAR® electric anchor windlass, and a 25kg polished SS anchor on 60m of 8mm HT chain. Built for genuine offshore use.

vs production yachts: offshore-spec · redundant bilge system

Real-World Use Cases

Every Occasion.
Every Horizon.

The H420's layout, range, and comfort specification make it one of the most versatile vessels in its class. Here's how serious owners use it.

⚓
Extended Coastal Cruising

With 400 gallons of fuel capacity and genuine offshore hull strength, the H420 handles multi-day passages along coastlines — from the Strait of Hormuz to the Mediterranean — with a level of confidence that few 42-foot production yachts can match. The twin outboard configuration provides redundancy and peace of mind far from port.

Recommended config: Twin 300HP Mercury · Full 400-gal fuel · Watermaker optional upgrade

🏖️
Family Offshore Vacation

Two private cabins with high-quality foam mattresses, two fully equipped heads with GROHE® fittings, four zones of 16,000 BTU air conditioning, and a galley stocked to match a shoreside kitchen — the H420 delivers five-star comfort for extended family getaways without the crew overhead of a larger motoryacht.

Sleeps 4 adults comfortably · AC throughout · Full galley · Swim platform

💼
Corporate & Client Entertainment

The panoramic salon, U-shaped dinette, cockpit wet bar, and fly bridge entertainment space give you multiple hosting environments for business networking, client reception, or VIP day events. The vessel projects authority and refinement — a business asset that doubles as a lifestyle statement.

Fly bridge helm + entertaining · Cockpit wet bar · Premium interior finishes

🎣
Serious Offshore Sport Fishing

The 0.6M draft opens shallow reef and bank fishing beyond the reach of heavier hull alternatives. The twin outboard configuration provides the speed to reach far-offshore grounds efficiently, while the self-draining deck, cockpit wash-down outlet, retractable swim ladder, and structural rigidity keep working conditions safe and practical.

Shallow draft · 0.6M · High-speed run to grounds · Deck wash · Stable working platform

Quality & Craftsmanship

Built to Standards.
Trusted by Professionals.

The H420 specifies commercial and military-grade components throughout — not because it's necessary for a leisure boat, but because the original designers believed owners deserve genuine reliability.

5083 Marine Aluminum Grade

H116 temper, magnesium-alloyed aluminum — the marine construction standard for commercial vessels, naval craft, and offshore patrol boats. Superior corrosion resistance in saltwater environments with no osmosis risk.

6× Bilge Pump Redundancy

Six automatic RULE® 1100GPH bilge pumps with float switches plus two manual emergency backups. The kind of redundancy that reflects a genuine offshore safety philosophy, not a minimum regulatory requirement.

4× 16k BTU AC Zones

Four independent 16,000 BTU reverse-cycle marine air conditioning units — standard fit. In tropical and Middle Eastern waters, this is the difference between a liveable vessel and an uncomfortable one. Most 42' yachts offer one or two zones optionally.
